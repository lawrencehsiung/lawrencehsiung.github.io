Iced pour-over · James Hoffmann inspired

Iced V60

Coffee
16 g
Hot water
150 g
Ice
100 g
Time
~2:30
  1. Put 100 g of ice in the server and grind a little finer than you would for hot V60.
  2. Bloom with about 35 g of water, swirl, and wait 45 seconds.
  3. Pour slowly and steadily up to 150 g.
  4. Swirl and let it draw down, then swirl the server until the ice melts. Serve over fresh ice.

My tweak: a drop or two of salt water if it tastes bitter or flat.

Immersion pour-over · Deepak Talwar inspired

Clever Dripper

Coffee
16 g
Water
250 g
Grind
Medium
Time
~3:30
  1. Pour 250 g of just-off-boil water into the Clever first.
  2. Add the coffee and stir gently until all the grounds are wet.
  3. Steep for 2 minutes, then stir to break the crust.
  4. Wait 30 seconds, then set it on your cup and let it drain.

Honey haze

Base
Pour-over
Honey
1 tsp
Milk
A splash
Serve
Chilled
  1. Brew a pour-over and chill it.
  2. Dissolve 1 tsp of honey in a little hot water and stir it into the coffee.
  3. Pour over ice and top with a little frothed milk.
  4. Finish with a drizzle of honey.
Espresso · on the La Marzocco at work

Classic latte

Dose
18 g
Yield
36 g
Shot
25–30 s
Milk
~180 ml
  1. Pull a double shot: 18 g in, 36 g out, in 25 to 30 seconds.
  2. Steam milk to about 60 °C with a thin layer of silky microfoam.
  3. Tap and swirl the pitcher, then pour into the center of the shot.
