Hey, I'm Lawrence! 👋

I'm a multidisciplinary engineer with a passion for design and film photography.

I love building things that solve problems and tell stories. Some of my favorite professional projects include architecting and integrating the electrical systems for the upcoming Lucid Cosmos and Earth show cars, designing and executing hardware tests for the Farmwise Vulcan autonomous smart weeding robot, certifying consumer audio hardware at Zoom, and validating hardware for the Versa Series at Fitbit.

I graduated from UC San Diego in 2018 with a B.S. in Electrical Engineering and a minor in Business Studies, and from San Jose State University in 2021 with an M.S. in Electrical Engineering focusing on machine learning and power systems.

In my free time, I like to explore new coffee recipes ☕, play badminton 🏸, or complete the daily crossword ✏️! I love talking about the latest in wearable tech, sustainable transportation, and consumer electronics.
